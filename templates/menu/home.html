Our Story

Born from a passion for the perfect cup, The Hearth Roastery has been a local cornerstone for over a decade. We source ethically grown beans from small farms globally, roasting them in-house daily to ensure peak freshness.

Our space is designed to be your second living room—a cozy, tactile environment where the aroma of espresso and fresh baking provides a backdrop for connection and reflection.
